**(0:15 - 0:45) The Setup**

I then asked 10 participants to blindly rate the outputs on Cultural Authenticity, Realism, and Prompt Adherence via a simple rating UI, which I’ve mocked up in my submission."

"Here is what I found: Models really struggle with 'ordinary'. DALL-E 3 followed the prompt perfectly but the images looked like an expensive, hyper-saturated studio ad—missing the 'candid smartphone' requirement completely. Gemini 3.1 Flash Preview was the clear winner for Realism; it actually captured the gritty, natural lighting of a phone camera and accurate architectural details like standard Indian window grills."

**(1:15 - 1:45) Why It Matters**
"Why does this matter to an AI lab building for India? Because 'good enough for global' is not 'accurate enough for local'. An Indian AI lab needs to solve the long-tail of cultural nuances—like the difference between a casual Mumbai balcony and a Delhi one. The ability to generate this 'Bharat Realism' without hallucinating stereotypes is exactly what enterprise clients in Indian retail and advertising are willing to pay for. Thanks for watching."
